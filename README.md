## 🚀 Selected Projects

Here's a curated selection of my academic and research projects:

### 🗺️ [Interactive Dashboard: NYC Vision Zero — Traffic Calming & Spatial Spillover](https://github.com/FedericoTrionfetti/Interactive-Dashboard-NYC-Streets_Multi-Level-Evaluating-Urban-Traffic-Calming-and-Spatial-Spillover)
> **[Master's Thesis | Python, Flask, DC.js, Mapbox, Parquet]**  
A full-stack interactive dashboard built to answer two core research questions: *Where* does NYC invest in road safety, and *how effectively does it actually work?*  
Introduces a 3-level progressive analysis workflow (Macro Network → Cohort Evaluation → Micro Spillover Analysis), using a **Risk Rate** metric (seasonally adjusted crashes normalized by AADT) to isolate true safety gains from mere traffic shifts. The system explicitly measures whether interventions improve safety or displace crashes onto adjacent streets.

---

### 🤖 [LLM-Supported Ontology Generation — Honor Program Research Project](https://github.com/FedericoTrionfetti/Honor_Program_La_Sapienza_Roma_LLM_Supported_Ontology_Generation)
> **[Honor Program @ Sapienza | Python, NLP, LLMs, Knowledge Graphs]**  
Research project investigating how Large Language Models can support and semi-automate the generation of formal ontologies from unstructured text. Explores prompt engineering strategies, entity extraction, relation classification, and the alignment of LLM outputs with structured knowledge representation formats. A concrete application of Generative AI to knowledge engineering.

---

### 🦠 [Time Series Forecasting of COVID-19 Vaccination via Google Trends](https://github.com/FedericoTrionfetti/Time-Series-Forecasting-of-COVID19-Vaccination-using-Machine-Learning-and-Google-Trends)
> **[Big Data for Official Statistics | Python, XGBoost, Random Forest, MLP]**  
Investigates whether Google search behaviour (at a 29-day lag) can predict vaccination rates across 360 parallel regional time series. Introduces a **Progressive Normalization Algorithm** to eliminate look-ahead bias in Google Trends data. A "Blind Test" (removing all autoregressive features) proved that Google Trends is a powerful, independent signal—not statistical noise—becoming the #1 predictor in tree-based models.

---

### 🧠 [GPT Implementation with KV-Cache and Heavy Hitter Compression in JAX](https://github.com/FedericoTrionfetti/Neural-Networks_GPT-Implementation-with-KV-Cache-Handling-and-Heavy-Hitter-Compression-in-JAX)
> **[Neural Networks for Data Science | Python, JAX, Flax]**  
From-scratch implementation of a GPT-style transformer and the H₂O (Heavy Hitter Oracle) algorithm for KV-cache compression, enabling memory-efficient inference without significant accuracy loss. Implemented entirely in JAX for accelerator-agnostic execution.

---

### 🛢️ [SQL to NoSQL: Airbnb Dataset Migration from MySQL to MongoDB](https://github.com/FedericoTrionfetti/From-Relational-Model-to-NoSQL-Migration-and-Restructuring-of-Airbnb-Dataset-into-MongoDB)
> **[Data Management for Data Science | SQL, JavaScript, MongoDB]**  
Full migration pipeline from a relational Airbnb database (MySQL) to a MongoDB document model, including schema redesign, data restructuring, and NoSQL query implementation.

---
### 🕸️ [Graph Neural Networks: Multi-Head GAT & Graph Pooling](https://github.com/FedericoTrionfetti/GraphNeuralNetwork-Attention-and-Pooling-Layers-Multi-Head-GAT-and-Graph-Pooling-Layers)
> **[Neural Networks for Data Science | Python, PyTorch Geometric]**  
Contrasts **Transductive** (Cora) vs **Inductive** (PPI) learning with Multi-Head Graph Attention Networks, and benchmarks **EdgePool** vs **SAGPool** for graph classification on the PROTEINS dataset.

### 📈 [Bayesian vs Frequentist Regression Study](https://github.com/FedericoTrionfetti/Bayesian-vs-Frequentist-Regression-Study)
> **[Statistics for Data Science | R, JAGS, rjags, ggmcmc]**  
Comparative study of Full Normal, Reduced Normal, **Beta Regression**, and Hierarchical (school-level random intercepts) Bayesian models against a classical OLS baseline to explain student performance (`G3`). Full MCMC diagnostic suite: trace plots, ESS, posterior predictive checks.

### 🏦 [Quantitative Finance: Options Pricing & Hedging (Black-Scholes & Monte Carlo)](https://github.com/FedericoTrionfetti/Applied-Statistics-Quantitative-Finance_Options-Pricing-and-Hedging-Analysis_Black-Scholes-and-MC)
